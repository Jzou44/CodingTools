Whois with or without www prefix?

Notice: In most situation, whois with a secondary domain will not return any result. if you have no luck with your query, try to remove www. in the front and try again.

Why Whois?

You found a cool website and want to chat with the website owner but can't find website owner's phone number or Email address. Whois is the tool to find domain owner's contact information.

Whois tool is started from 1982, it helps law enforcement agents to identify domain owner's authority. Nowadays, if you pay Godaddy an extra fee, Godaddy can help you to hide your contact information, so your phone number and email address will not appear when others do a whois search on your domain.

Should I hide my contact information in Whois search result?

For regular website, I suggest people do not hide their contact information in whois, because it represents your website's reputation and you don't need to pay a costly annual fee. However, if your privacy is very important to you or you want to stay away from potential spam, whois privacy protection can be a worthwhile investment.
